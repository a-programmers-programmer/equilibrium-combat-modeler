Regular Relics: Tier 1
Pros:
-Incredibly good for afk - chilling, working from home, family stuff
-Autobanking is nice
-Don’t need perfect plus or stone spirits for mining
-Upgraded resources could be nice, either for food or for more xp when processing. You also get a head start on your next tier of item when you reach the proper level. For example, when mining mith and working towards making adamant gear you will have some resources already.
Cons:
-Doesn’t give you tools but you can smith decent ones pretty easily
-Would need to press space bar when you get an archaeology artefact but you could probably hold it down with a rock while afk (possibly against the rules)
-Will probably or maybe have access to porters later which devalues infinite bank a smidge
Other:
-Could be a potential relic to reload into
